Ava privacy policy

Ava is a personal assistant with exactly one user, its owner. With the owner's consent it accesses the owner's own Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive to answer the owner's requests.

Google user data is used only to serve the owner. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Data stays on the owner's own computer except when processed to answer the owner's requests.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
